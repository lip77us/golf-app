Why par and yards are locked

A re-index does not change the golf course, so the rating and slope still describe it and every handicap in the event stays correct.

Change a yardage and they do not — the rating would describe a course that does not exist, and every net score in the field would be quietly wrong. That is a course correction, not a local override.
