Leaderboard — Nassau

One card per match. Team identity is colour (Blue vs Orange), never “T1/T2”. Three bets — Front 9 / Back 9 / Overall — read in match-play terms: N UP (live lead), N&M (decided early, close-out), AS (all square). A resolved plain-margin bet gets a · Final tag; “N&M” already implies closed. Presses list their hole range (Auto Press holes 3–9). With the Claremont variant the card splits into a Top (Nassau) dollar game and a Bottom 2-points game, each with its own bets, presses and settlement.
