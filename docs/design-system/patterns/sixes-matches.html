Sixes matches

Three 6-hole segments; the four players re-pair each segment so P1 partners everyone once. A horizontal strip of segment cards (rendered in score entry and on the leaderboard) — the active match centres itself, and the next match reveals only once the current one closes out. Status is coloured in the leading team’s colour; teams are blue (Team A) / orange (Team B).
