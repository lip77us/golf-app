How Triple Nassau works
Three players, three simultaneous one-on-one Nassaus — Paul v Dave, Paul v Sam, Dave v Sam. Each match runs the standard Front 9 / Back 9 / Overall bets off the same setup, so there is one stake, one handicap mode, and one press rule for all of them. Every hole you post is scored against both of your opponents at once; you can win one match and lose the other on the same hole.
