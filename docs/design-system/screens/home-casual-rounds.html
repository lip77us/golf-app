Rounds (home)

Matches the shipped list. Three rows per card, each earning its place:

  1. Course — date held right, with delete beneath it.
  2. Game and state — primary game, then side games while they fit; the list ellipsizes rather than wrapping.
  3. Players and result — you first with your gross, then the group, and the settlement right-justified at the end.

Watched rounds carry no trash can. Only the round’s owner can delete it, so the control is absent rather than disabled — and an eye replaces the money, since you had no stake.

Active shows live state where Completed shows the result: a pulsing dot with Through 12, or Not started. Row 3 drops the score — a partial gross tells you nothing mid-round — and carries the stake (skin in the game) instead, an em dash for a play-for-fun round. Settlement is a completed-round result: nothing is netted mid-round.

Cross-account rounds. A round a friend added you to (played or watched) doesn’t carry the group’s player names, so row 3 shows the group label in their place; watched rounds still get the eye. Only nettable games settle — a Nassau or Rabbit round shows the gross with no money, exactly as on the Settlement tab.

Watched filter. Include watched is disabled when there are no watched rounds to reveal, and the count collapses to a plain total — a live control that changes nothing is worse than a dead one. Completed defaults to Rounds I played; the count on the right (“5 of 7”) keeps hidden rounds honest rather than silently missing. Active is unfiltered — following a live round is the point there, so the filter row is absent, not empty.
