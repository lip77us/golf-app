# Play Store listing copy — Halved Golf

Google Play equivalent of `app-store-listing.md`. Same rules apply: keep
gambling-trigger words OUT (no "bet/wager/gambling/winnings/real money"); use
"stakes/scoring/settle up". Only list shipping, enabled games.

**Key difference from Apple: Play has no keywords field.** The short and full
descriptions are what Play indexes for search, so the terms that live in
Apple's `Keywords` (banker, vegas, wolf, sixes, stableford, spots, handicap,
foursome, matchplay, scorecard, tournament, survivor) have to appear in the
prose here instead.

## Full description (4000 max)

Halved keeps score for the games golfers really play. Set up a round, pick your
games, enter scores hole by hole, and Halved does the rest — live results,
handicaps, a shareable spectator page, and an easy settle-up at the end.

GAMES YOU CAN PLAY

• Banker — one golfer takes the hole on against the other three, each in its own
match, with doubles called at a ball in the air
• Sequoya 3s — six three-hole matches, partners rotating every third hole
• Skins, with optional carryover
• Nassau — front, back, overall, with presses
• Triple Nassau — three 1-on-1 Nassaus at once
• Las Vegas — 2v2 team game
• Sixes — 2v2 best ball with rotating partners
• Fourball — 2v2 best-ball match play over eighteen
• Better Ball — the best of your team's balls counts on every hole
• Wolf
• Rabbit
• Honors — hold the honor and score a point every hole you keep it
• Survivor — three players, last one standing takes the hole
• Points 5-3-1
• Stableford, with a custom points table
• Spots — a side game for one-putts, sandies, barkies and more
• Match Play, Stroke Play and Quota Nassau
• Multi-Group Skins across foursomes
• One-round team cup
• Scramble and Shamble team events
• Two-golfer teams — best ball, alternate shot, Scotch, Chapman

CASUAL ROUNDS

Playing with friends? Start a round, choose one or more games, and assign
players and tees. Halved tracks every hole and shows who's ahead in real time.

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND, WITHOUT LEAVING THE HOLE

Every game carries a line under its title saying where you stand right now —
your match, your place in the field, your points, who is holding it — and each
game answers the question it actually makes you ask on the tee. One tap opens
the full leaderboard. On an iPhone the same board runs on your lock screen, and
tells you when you get a stroke on the hole you are walking to.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS

Add a golfer by phone number and send a text invite in one tap. When a friend
adds you to a round — or invites you to watch one — it shows up on your own
Casual Rounds and Tournaments lists, clearly flagged so you know whether you're
playing or just following along.

GROUP CHAT AND LIVE FEED

Every round has a group chat and a live feed that calls out birdies and key
moments as they happen — so the whole group can follow the action, even players
in other groups.

TOURNAMENTS

Running something bigger? Halved supports multiple foursomes, several games at
once, and multi-round events — with live leaderboards, team cup play, and
formats like Irish Rumble, Pink Ball and Singles. Team events run small teams or
two-golfer pairs against the whole field on one board, each format with the
allowance it is actually played off. Split a field into flights and each one is
ranked and paid on its own.

FOLLOW A ROUND YOU ARE NOT PLAYING

Share a round link and anyone can open a live page — no account, no install.
Every game draws its own board there, and it refreshes while the group plays.

FIND YOUR COURSE FAST

Just start typing a course name and pick it from one list — your own courses, a
shared catalog, and a full course database, all in a single search.

HANDICAPS YOUR WAY

Score net (with an adjustable handicap percentage), gross, or
strokes-off-the-low-player — set per game to match how your group plays. Every
scorecard shows exactly where each player gets a stroke, hole by hole, so the
handicap is never a mystery.

SETTLE UP

At the end of the round, the Settlement tab nets every game into one summary and
shows exactly who owes whom — plus the fewest payments to square up. Settle in
seconds, with no spreadsheets and no napkin math.

Halved is built for friendly play among friends. It tracks informal stakes for
scoring purposes only and does not process payments.
