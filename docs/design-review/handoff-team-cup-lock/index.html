Team Cup Lock Screen

The one card every golfer carries on the lock screen during a Triple-Cup cup. The big number is the whole cup: Red against Blue, across every foursome, updating whichever group wins a point. Your own group's four points ride underneath as a sub-total.

How to read this

This documents what is built

Every mockup below is drawn from a real payload generated by the server, for a two-group Red v. Blue cup, not from a design file. Sizes are the Swift's own: 1 CSS pixel here is 1 point on the phone, and the card is 360pt wide. The widget renders in SF, so the mockups do too; the packet's Schibsted Grotesk and Spline Sans are not in the widget bundle yet (see the constraints section).

The configuration follows the cup

There is no setting. The same triple_cup builder draws a casual four-point Triple Cup or this team card, and it switches to the team card whenever the round carries a cup config. Six slots change meaning; none change position.

States

What the card says, through the day

Group 1's Red player is the reader unless noted. One Blue reader is included, because the only personal slot is your-group line and its dot; everything else is identical on every phone.

Alerts

Two pushes, and nothing else

Every other score moves the card silently. In a cup, points land in groups an hour ahead, which is the one case a push is worth its interruption. But a cup can carry many points, and a golfer who switches alerts off loses the few that mattered, so there are exactly two.

EventTitle (real output)Body
  • Decided outranks lead change. A clinch is also a lead change; one push, not two.
  • Level counts as a lead change (from somebody to nobody). A second point that keeps it level does not.
  • Fires once per posted score, not once per phone, and remembers the last score it saw, so the clinch fires exactly once.
  • The body says what is still out rather than narrating. Group 2 took their Foursomes cannot be derived from the data, and a push that guesses is wrong on a lock screen.
Constraints

What a redesign has to live inside

  • ~160pt tall. iOS clips a lock-screen Live Activity beyond roughly 160pt. The sides line is one row everywhere for this reason: a row for each live Singles measured 163pt on its own.
  • The contract is a set of required keys. header, number, sides, state, pips, footer, and inside the needle blue and orange. A payload missing one of them does not fail loudly: APNs accepts it, the phone cannot decode it, and iOS drops the whole update. That is what took every card down in 2.9.0. Rename or remove any of these and every installed phone goes dark. New fields must be optional.
  • The needle keys are positional. blue is the share drawn from the left, orange from the right. Changing which colour sits where is a Swift change and needs a build.
  • Always-on dims the whole card to roughly 74% brightness. Anything that carries meaning at 55% white or below is at risk under it.
  • Fonts. The widget is a separate process with its own bundle and cannot reach the app's downloaded Google Fonts, so it renders SF. Shipping the packet's faces means bundling the .ttf files into the widget target; both families are routed through one function, so the swap is two lines.
  • The stroke ribbon. When the reader gets a stroke on the hole in play, a gold POPPING ON HOLE 7 band sits above the header. It is shared by every card, and on a net cup it will fire often.
For design

Open questions

Headline order and needle side disagree Decide

The headline reads team 1 first (Red–Blue: 2–1, Red on the left). The needle draws blue from the left, so on a Red v. Blue cup Red's number is on the left and Red's bar is on the right. Colour still carries the meaning, but the two elements point opposite ways. Options: order the headline to match the needle (server only, but team 1 then reads second), or teach the needle to follow the headline (needs a build). For 10 Oct: set Blue up as team 1 and both line up with no change.

The push writes the score team-1-first Decide

Blue take the lead — 0–1 reads backwards: the leader's name, then a score that looks like they are losing. Writing the score leader-first (Blue lead 1–0) is a server-only copy change. Same fix as above if Blue is team 1.

Ribbon plus team card is unmeasured Measure

No height has been recorded for this card. The Survivor card grew 18pt when the ribbon appeared (135 to 153). If the team card sits in the mid-140s, which is an estimate from the layout code and not a measurement, the ribbon could push it past 160 and clip the footer. Measure on a device before 10 Oct, with and without a stroke on the hole.

Which number is whose? Decide

The headline carries no team label. 2–1 in orange says orange leads, but a golfer has to know team 1 is written first to know which digit is his. Team initials or colouring each digit separately would answer it, at some width.

Red wears orange Decided · shipped

Confirmed 22 Sep 2026: orange reads better on the glass than a lifted red, and needs no build.
