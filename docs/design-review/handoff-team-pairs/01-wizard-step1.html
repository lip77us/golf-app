Team Play — the third shape

The app already knows two tournament shapes. Cup is two big sides and the unit is a match. Individual is a field of singles and the unit is a card. Team Play is the third: many small teams, one leaderboard, one score per team per hole.

It is the shape a club actually runs most often — a Saturday scramble with six teams and a pot — and it is the simplest of the three, so the wizard should feel shorter, not longer.

Format is chosen here, not later

Picking Team Play does not tell the app how a team makes a score. Scramble and shamble need different entry screens and different handicap maths, so the format question comes immediately after — it is the first thing every other step reads.

One day, and the wizard does not ask

The Cup and Individual flows are both multi-day. This one is one round, stated rather than chosen. A club that wants two days runs two tournaments; carrying best-N counting, dropped rounds and a cross-round board into a Saturday scramble buys nothing and costs three screens.

RoundsFixed at one. Nothing downstream has a round dimension — no round columns, no counting rule, no per-round pot.
Side gamesOff by default. In a scramble the team is the foursome, so there is no field to run a ball game against. A foursome that wants a bet among its own men still has the casual games.
MoneyOne pool — team championship. Flat entry per golfer, taken at signup, same as Individual.

Field size sets the team count, not the reverse

The TD enters golfers and the app says six teams, one of three. Asking him for a team count first makes him do division at the first-tee before he has a headcount.

Fours or pairs is a control, not a format

Two-man tournaments are this same flow with the size set to two. It changes the format list on step 3 and the allowance table on step 5 — nothing else. A separate “Two-Man Tournament” entry in the list above would duplicate seven screens to change two.

Nothing here is disabled without saying why

House rule, inherited from the Cup. Next is live from the moment the three defaults are on screen — the TD can accept a one-round scramble by tapping once.
