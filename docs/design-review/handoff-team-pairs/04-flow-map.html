Team Play — the flow

The third tournament shape: many small teams, one round, one leaderboard. Eight setup steps — two of them existing screens reused unchanged — three play surfaces, one pool. It is deliberately the shortest of the three flows — a Saturday scramble is the most common event a club runs and the least complicated thing it does.

Setup — eight steps

STEP 1
FORMAT

Tournament format

Cup, Individual or Team Play. Rounds is stated as one, not offered. Golfers in, entry set.

Decides
Which wizard runs, and that nothing downstream has a round dimension.
Watch for
Field size sets the team count, not the reverse. 23 golfers → six teams, one of three.
STEP 2
COURSE

Course & tees

The existing Set tees screen, unchanged. Pre-suggested by sex, confirmed per golfer, Set all per group.

Decides
Every golfer’s course handicap — which is what the allowance on step 5 is a percentage of.
Watch for
Nothing new was built here. A team round needs tees for exactly the reason a casual round does.
STEP 3
FORMAT

How a team scores

Fours: scramble or shamble, with the shamble expanding in place to the ball-count controls. Pairs: scramble, best ball, alternate shot, Scotch or Chapman.

Decides
The scorecard, the allowance table, and how many of the scores count.
Watch for
Locked once a score lands. No second rules screen — the extras live under the radio.
STEP 4
DRIVES

Drive requirement

Scramble and shamble alike. None, per nine, per eighteen, or alternating pairs — three quotas and one schedule. A three-man team runs AB / BC / AC.

Decides
Whether the card gains a drive row, and what the tracker is counting toward.
Watch for
Per nine has two windows. The front does not carry to the back.
STEP 5
HANDICAP

Handicap & allowance

Net or gross, then the allowance the format prescribes — 25/20/15/10 for a scramble, a percentage tracking the ball count for a shamble.

Decides
Every team’s figure, worked on his own men rather than on an example.
Watch for
Whole strokes, rounded once on the total — never per contribution, which costs a stroke.
STEP 6
TEAMS

Build teams · pairs

The TD assigns all 23 by hand. Allowance appears per team as it fills; a balance strip shows the spread. The three-man team carries a phantom 4th row.

Decides
Who plays with whom, and — visibly — whether the pot was decided here.
Watch for
Next waits on an empty tray, not on balance. Uneven teams are allowed; unplaced golfers are not.
STEP 7
MONEY

Entry & payout

Fee per golfer, how many places pay, and the split. Every control shows its consequence in dollars — and in per-man dollars.

Decides
The pot and what each place is worth. Needs the field size and the team count, so it cannot come earlier.
Watch for
Save is blocked off 100%, with the shortfall named. Places are capped at the team count.
STEP 8
REVIEW

Review & create

The existing review step, unchanged. Format, drive rule, allowance, six teams, one pool.

Decides
Nothing new. It is the last chance to change the format before it locks.

Play — three surfaces

ON THE COURSE

Score entry

A stepper and one big number for a scramble; the four-man grid with counting scores tinted for a shamble. The drive row sits on both.

Decides
Nothing. It records — but it is where the drive warning fires, two holes before the quota fails.
Watch for
Anyone on the team can enter. No designated scorer, or the board is dark until 18.
ON THE COURSE

Drive tracker

Off the card. Per-window pips for each golfer, and the pair rota — set by the team on the 1st tee — if the rule is alternating.

Decides
Whether the team can still satisfy the rule. Warns; never blocks.
Watch for
Falling short costs nothing by default. The penalty is opt-in.
AFTER

Leaderboard

Six rows, ranked on whole-stroke net, ties marked T and drawn adjacent. Teams still out are marked. The pool sits under the board, italic until everyone signs.

Decides
The order. There is nothing else on it — no tabs, because there is nothing to tab to.
Watch for
Ties are the normal case now. Combine the places and split; no countback.
AFTER

Settlement → receipt

One pool, three places, 50/30/20, each expanded to the men who get paid. Tied teams combine their places and split what those places pay — and a three-man team divides its share three ways, because the phantom 4th earned the strokes and cannot be paid. Odd cents go to the team’s highest course handicap so the pool balances to zero. Gated on all six teams signed. The receipt and the texting flow are the Individual ones, reused unchanged — a team receipt is simply a short one.

What this flow deliberately does not have

Left out
Multiple roundsA club wanting two days runs two tournaments. Best-N counting, round columns and a cross-round board would cost three screens and buy nothing.
Side gamesThe team is the foursome, so there is no field to run a ball game against. A foursome that wants a bet among its own men still has the casual games.
Borrowed golfersA borrowed man would hit shots for a team he is competing against. A three-man team fields a phantom 4th instead — handicapped at the average of the three, its ball played by whoever is not driving.
Still open
FlightsDeferred with the Individual flow. Six teams is under the size where flighting means anything.
Extra formatsIrish Rumble, modified counts, best-ball variants — all of them are a shamble with the count moved. A club that wants Irish Rumble has it casually and as an Individual side game.
TiesCombined and split — expected, not exceptional, now the handicap is a whole number. No countback and no card-off, matching the Individual flow.
