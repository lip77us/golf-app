Building the pairs

The same screen as the four-man builder, with two men to a card instead of four. Manual assignment, live allowance, a balance strip, and Next waiting on an empty tray.

Balance matters more in pairs, not less

Four handicaps average out; two do not. A scramble pair of 3 and 22 plays off 4 and a pair of 9 and 23 plays off 7 — three strokes, from two men each, on a card where the field will finish inside six.

So the strip is the same and the argument is stronger. The TD is not being told what to do; he is being told what he just did.

The percentage is positional, and with two men it is stark

In a scramble the low man takes 35% and the high man 15%. Swap a pair’s two members between teams and both figures move — there is no third or fourth man to absorb it. Each row carries its own contribution for that reason.

The field must be even

Thirteen golfers is twelve and a problem. A phantom partner is not the answer: in fours the phantom is a handicap device for a team that still hits four balls, and in pairs it would be an imaginary man taking half the shots in an alternate shot.

So the block is plain. Next names the unpaired golfer rather than reporting a count, because the fix is to add or remove one man and the TD needs to know which one is standing there. Three ways out, offered on the block itself — nothing is disabled without saying why.

Add a golferThe usual answer. The TD partners the odd man himself.
Remove oneHe sits out or plays a casual round alongside.
Play a threeBest ball only. A third ball is just another option to count; in alternate shot and Chapman it is structurally impossible, and in a scramble it is a straight advantage.

The three-man option is therefore hidden unless the format is best ball, and even then it carries its allowance adjustment on the row. Offering a choice that three of the four formats cannot honour would be worse than not offering it.

Names default to the two surnames

A pair is Maiolini & Yau before it is Pine. Colour names are right for fours, where four surnames will not fit anywhere; two fit on a leaderboard row, and golfers refer to a pair that way out loud. Free text over it, sixteen characters, colour still assigned for the card.

Everything else is the four-man screen

Drag to move, tray at the bottom with a count, empty seats named rather than blank, uneven allowed and unplaced not.
