Pairs

Two-man teams are not a fourth tournament shape. They are Team Play with the team size set to two: the same wizard, the same leaderboard, the same one pool, the same settlement. Only two steps behave differently, and both do so because the formats are different, not because the shape is.

Everything the four-man flow decided still holds — one round, no side games, whole-stroke handicaps, ties combine and split, the TD sets fee and places himself. None of it is re-litigated here.

Team size is one control on step 1

Fours or pairs. It changes the format list on step 3 and the allowance table on step 5, and nothing else. A separate “Two-Man Tournament” entry in the format list would duplicate seven screens to change two.

The allowance is doing enormous work here

This is the finding worth acting on. The same pair — Maiolini 4 and Yau 19 — plays off 4 in a scramble and 12 in alternate shot. Three times the strokes, for the same two men, purely from the format.

Scramble35% low + 15% high. Two balls every shot, so the pair is strong and takes almost nothing.
Best ball85% each, own ball. Not a team figure — each man plays his own strokes and the better net counts.
Alternate shot50% of the combined. One ball, both mistakes. The most generous by a distance, and correctly so.
Scotch60% low + 40% high. Both drive, take the better, then alternate.
Chapman60% low + 40% high. Both drive, swap for the second, then one ball — the same table as Scotch.

Scotch and Chapman share an allowance, and that is the honest answer rather than a manufactured difference. Both are two drives and then one ball; Chapman buys one extra shot of position, which is not worth a stroke.

So the format screen shows the pair’s figure on every option, before it is chosen. A TD picking Chapman because it sounds fun should see that it doubles his field’s strokes against a scramble.

Two scorecards, and only one of them has two numbers

Scramble, alternate shot, Scotch and Chapman all end in one ball, so they take the one-number card. Best ball is the only pairs format that enters two scores, with the counting one tinted — the same card the shamble uses, with two rows instead of four.

In Scotch the drive tap is an instruction, not a record

Every format that chooses a tee shot has the same control on the card. What it does differs, and Scotch is the case worth building for.

In a scramble, tapping whose drive you took records compliance against a quota. In Scotch it tells the pair who hits next — the partner whose drive was not chosen plays the second shot. So the card answers with a sentence rather than a tick: Maiolini plays the second shot, then alternate.

The quota is still available on top, because the tap is already there and some groups require a drive count. It is optional and off by default.

Alternate shot needs a rota, not a quota

Who tees off on the odd holes is the rule, not a preference: the pair sets it on the 1st tee and it is fixed for eighteen. Same mechanic as alternating pairs in the four-man flow, and the same reason — a rota that can be re-cut mid-round is not a rota.

It also means the app must state it on every tee. A pair that loses track plays a hole out of order and the round is gone.

ScrambleOptional drive minimum, as in the four-man flow. Usually one drive each per nine — two men and eighteen holes is a lot of slack.
Best ballNone. Both play their own ball off every tee.
Alternate shotA fixed odd/even tee rota. Not a quota — there is nothing to fall short of.
ScotchA drive tap on every hole, because it decides who plays next. An optional quota on top of it.
ChapmanNone. Both drive on every hole by definition.

The field must be even, and the screen names who is not paired

A phantom partner is not the answer here. In fours the phantom is a handicap device for a team that still hits four balls; in pairs it would be an imaginary man taking half the shots in an alternate shot. Pairs require an even field, the block names the unpaired golfer, and the TD adds or removes one — a ten-second fix that does not deserve a special case.

What is unchanged

Score picker, leaderboard card, payout screen, settlement, receipt. A pair is what sits in the name column.
