Two cards, one format apart

Everything else in the app enters a score per golfer. A scramble does not have one. Four men make one number, and pretending otherwise — four boxes, three of them ignored — is the single easiest way to get a scramble card wrong.

Scramble: one number, the size of the screen

The card is a stepper, not a grid. There is one value on the hole and it should be the largest thing on the phone, because it is being tapped by a man standing on the next tee holding a beer.

Par sits next to it and the value shows what it is — birdie, par, bogey — not just the digit. On a scramble everyone knows the number relative to par before they know the number.

DefaultOpens on par, not blank. A scramble team makes par more than any other score, and a wrong default beats no default when the alternative is a keypad.
DrivesOnly if the TD set a requirement. Then one row: whose drive did you take, with anyone short marked and the arithmetic done live. Off, the row does not exist.
StripThe eighteen holes across the bottom, filled ones solid. Position in the round without leaving the card.

Shamble: four scores, and the card says which counted

Same grid as every other four-man card in the app, with one addition that matters more than it looks: the counting scores are tinted and the non-counting ones greyed, live, as they are entered.

Best two of four means two men’s cards do nothing on a given hole. A man who shot 5 needs to see instantly that his 5 was not used — otherwise the team total looks wrong and someone re-enters it.

The count is stated on every hole

2 of 4 count in the header, on all eighteen holes. It does not change, and saying so every hole costs one line and settles the recurring question at the green.

The drive row is on both cards

A shamble picks a tee shot exactly as a scramble does, so the requirement, the tracker and the warning are identical. The only difference is where the row sits — above the four scores rather than below the one.

The drive row warns on the tee, not at the scoring table

Two men owing a drive with two holes left is still fine; with one hole left it is not. The row does that subtraction on every hole and says the consequence in a sentence — taking Detomasi’s drive here leaves the front nine short — because the moment a quota becomes unsatisfiable is invisible to four men who have had a few.

It never blocks the tap. The team may knowingly take the shortfall, and by default a shortfall costs nothing.

The count is stated on every hole

1 of 4 counts in the header, changing at 7 and again at 13. Irish Rumble’s whole character is the escalation, and a team that forgets it is on three balls plays the last six holes wrong.

Net is not shown on the card

A scramble handicap is one whole-number team figure applied to the round, not a stroke on a hole. Showing 6 on the card would invite subtracting it per hole. Gross on the card, net on the leaderboard, and the header names the allowance so it is not hidden.

Anyone on the team can enter

No designated scorer. Six teams entering their own hole means the leaderboard is live; making one man responsible means the board is dark until he catches up on 18.
