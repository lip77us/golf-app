Entry and payout

Three numbers, and they are the only ones anybody argues about after the round: what it costs, how many teams cash, and how the pot divides. They belong on one screen because changing any of them changes the other two’s meaning.

Worked, not described

Every control shows its consequence in dollars as it moves. Raise the fee and the pot moves; add a place and every share shrinks; drag 50 down to 40 and the other rows show what they now pay. A TD setting 50/30/20 is not choosing percentages, he is choosing what third place gets — so the screen shows that, and the percentage is the mechanism.

Per man is the number that matters

$287.50 to a team means nothing until it is divided. Each place shows the per-golfer share underneath, because that is what a man decides by: whether it is worth $25 to maybe win $72.

FeePer golfer, flat, taken at signup. Stepped in $5 — nobody charges $23.
Places1 to 4, capped at the team count. Six teams paying five places is a raffle.
SplitPresets first, custom underneath. Most TDs want 50/30/20 and should be able to tap once.

It must total 100, and Save says so

A split that adds to 95 leaves $28.75 in the TD’s pocket with no line explaining it. The total is shown live, turns amber off 100, and Save is blocked with the reason on the button — the house rule from the Cup work: nothing is disabled without saying why.

Rounding is not the TD’s problem. He works in whole percentages; the app handles the cents, and the settlement screen states where the odd ones went.

Half the field is the advice, not the rule

Six teams and three places means half of them cash, which is what keeps a Saturday scramble worth entering. The screen says so under the places control. It does not enforce it — winner-takes-all is a legitimate choice and the preset is there.

Ties are warned about here, where they are cheap

Whole-stroke handicaps tie often, and a tie combines places. One line explains that the number of places is the number of prizes, not the number of teams paid — two teams tied for the last paying place still split it. Better read here than discovered at the scoring table.

Where it sits

After teams, before review. It needs the field size to compute the pot and the team count to cap the places, and both are only known once the teams are built.
