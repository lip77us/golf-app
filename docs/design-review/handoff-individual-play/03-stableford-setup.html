Stableford setup, reused

The casual screen does the job already. Points table, three presets, six editable values, negatives allowed. A tournament asks exactly the same question, so it opens exactly this screen — step 3a, only when the method is Stableford.

Three things change in tournament context

ScopeA chip says what the table governs: this tournament, both rounds. Casual sets one round; a two-round event cannot have two scales.
MoneyHow the money settles is not asked here. The tournament pays through paid places on the Payout step. Per-point settlement makes the pot unknowable until the last card lands.
Preset stateThe active preset is selected, and editing any value flips it to Custom — live shows three chips with none marked.

Why per-point does not survive here

The same argument the Cup work settled for points per stroke: a per-point price means nobody can state the prize until the field finishes. A tournament advertises 1st / 2nd / 3rd at signup. Per point stays a casual and group-bet mechanism, where the table is four people who can do the arithmetic in the cart.

No floor, no validation of the table

Settled: the TD sets the points as he sees fit. Negatives, a 10 for an albatross, zero for par — all valid. The screen does not impose a floor or argue with an unusual table; it reports what the table implies and gets out of the way.

The cap and the zero

Standard scores double bogey or worse = 0, which already puts a floor under a blow-up hole — so the net double-bogey max changes nothing on a standard table and is not mentioned. On a table with negatives, it does bite, and the note on screen says so.

Save is not grey and silent

Live disables Save Configuration with no reason. Here it stays live, and if a field is empty the button names the row that is missing.
