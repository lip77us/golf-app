Individual play, step 1

This step already ships close to right. Live (1 of 7) asks type first, marks the two team types SOON, and offers Stroke play / Stableford underneath. So the changes here are small — a consequence strip, and a step list that counts honestly. The work is downstream: see the flow map.

Two methods, not three

Live ships stroke play as one format plus a Net Double-Bogey Cap toggle on the Handicap step, default on. That is a rule wearing a setting's clothes — the field cannot tell from a leaderboard which way it was set. With the cap always on, the toggle goes and the list stays two:

Stroke playNet double bogey max on every hole, always. Gross and net boards from the same card.
StablefordPoints against par, standard or modified scale — chosen per tournament on Scoring.

A capped hole is not a Stableford hole: the max keeps a blow-up from wrecking a net total, while Stableford stops counting the hole entirely. Both are drawn on the Scoring step so the difference is visible before the field tees off.

No strokes off

Live shows Net / Gross / SO Low on step 3 for Individual play. SO Low is a Cup mechanism and means nothing against a field — it must not render on this type. Individual play is net or gross with an allowance percentage.

Side games are field-wide

These are the tournament-scope games, and the only ones the TD sets. Foursome side bets — skins, Nassau, rabbit, survivor, sixes — belong to the foursome, are set up exactly as in a casual round, and read the tournament’s score entry rather than opening their own. A tournament foursome reads its rabbit off the leaderboard; the one thing that needs capturing on the hole is spots and junk.

Pink BallOne ball per group, no replacement. Last to lose it wins; if several groups finish 18 with the ball alive, low net breaks it.
Irish RumbleField-wide ranking of foursome nets — the Cup version scores your four only, this one ranks every group against every other.
Match Play FoursomeOne-day bracket among the foursomes, 9 holes per match, winner takes all. The casual game already implements it; reuse that engine.
Day betMulti-day only. Inherits the main game and pays each day separately. Anyone who wins 36-hole money is auto-DQ'd from it.

Entry is a flat fee per side game, taken at signup, so a golfer's entries are known before round one and the side-game step never has to reopen.

On this file the type list is drawn for context: Cup play links across to the Cup step, and the two team types are dimmed — their formats are drawn there, not here.

What the step list does

  • Stableford adds a step (the points scale). Stroke play does not — the max is not a question.
  • Day bet appears only when the event has more than one round. On this one-round count it is absent; the footnote says so rather than showing a struck row.
  • Flights are optional and off by default, so they ride on the Payout step rather than earning one.
  • Header count and panel count agree, as on the Cup step.

Still open

  • Whether Match Play Foursome brackets seed by handicap or by draw. The casual game draws at random; a tournament field may want seeding. Drawn both ways on the setup screen.
