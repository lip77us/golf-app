Scoring and handicap 8 decided

✓The net double-bogey cap is always onIt was a toggle in three places, which can disagree and cannot be read off a leaderboard. A rule, stated once where scores are entered, and shown on the card as a tinted cell.Scoring · Board
✓Stableford is chosen per tournament, not globallyA club’s Sunday game and its member-guest use different scales.Scoring
✓Stableford points reuse the casual setup screenSame question, same table. The tournament adds a scope chip for both rounds and drops per-point settlement.Stableford setup
✓The TD sets the Stableford points as he sees fitNegatives, an unusual scale, zero for par — all valid. No imposed floor and no argument with the table; the screen only reports what a negative table implies for the cap.Stableford setup
✓Strokes off low is wrong on the field, right on a match — and it is the bracket’s defaultMini Singles opens on strokes off low; the field games inherit full net. No low man to anchor to against a field. A match has exactly two players, so the bracket offers it — optional, full net by default.Bracket setup
✓The TD sets how many rounds count, on ScoringShown only when the tournament has more than two rounds — below that there is no answer worth asking for. All rounds, best 3 of 4, best 4 of 6; the board reads it and strikes the dropped column.Scoring
✓Net allowance defaults to 100%Not a club setting. Individual play is net or gross with an allowance the TD can move; it starts at full.Scoring
✓Flights are optional, off by default — and deferredNot implemented yet, so nothing downstream assumes them. When they land the intent is an even split of the field, not index ranges; they ride on the Payout step rather than earning one.Scoring

The games 18 decided

✓A halved match splits the money; only a trophy needs a nameSemis play on both days, back-calculated from hole 10. A halved final splits 1st and 2nd, with the trophy and the Sunday seat going to the last hole won — Paul won 11, Jim won 13, rest halved, Jim. A halved 3rd place simply splits, since nothing depends on the order.Bracket setup
✓A halved match plays onIt does not split. Play continues and the match is back-calculated against the finals pairing, reading back from hole 10 — which also removes the collision where sudden death borrowed the final’s first hole.Bracket setup
✓Seeds come from handicap, and the TD can drag themLowest index is seed 1 and meets seed 4. The order is overridable by drag, and the pairings derive from it — a TD who knows the field beats an index table.Bracket setup
✓Nobody waits for a halved matchThe resolved pair tee off on 10 on schedule and the match scores live under 1 UP vs. TBD. No ambiguity is possible: the pair in overtime can only halve, since the first hole they do not halve ends the semi and names the opponent.Bracket setup
✓Mini Singles runs on a field of 9 to 16Four groups is the ceiling. 13–16 is four groups and the full bracket; 9–12 is three and takes the short-field rule. Eight or fewer gives a final with no semis; over 16 needs a third day, so it is not offered on a two-day tournament rather than being faked with a play-in.Bracket setup
✓Sunday seeds by day 1 margin, then lowest indexNot index again — the man who won 5&4 takes seed 1 and meets the narrowest winner. Ties are ordinary, so the index breaker is not a formality.Bracket setup
✓A tie in the points round plays on, never a card-offTwo or three level after nine keep playing points until 1st and 2nd are clear, and the finals match is back-calculated from the 10th. Same mechanism as the halved match: the golf decides it.Bracket setup
✓A short field and a withdrawal take the same ruleSet once at setup, never asked on Sunday morning: promote the lowest net beaten finalist (default), points then a match over nine, or play it short-handed three or two.Bracket setup
✓Mini Singles is optional, at the TD’s discretionPlenty of stroke play tournaments run without it. Switched on at the side-game step — and nothing assumes it: no carve-out from the championship pool, no reserved day-2 foursome, unless it is on.Bracket setup
✓The bracket runs in every group on day 1, and the winners meet on day 2Four groups, four brackets, four champions — who play day 2 as one foursome for the title. Everyone else plays a normal stroke-play round. Run it as the main event instead and three quarters of the field is out of contention on Saturday.Bracket setup
✓Three groups: promote the best runner-up, or play pointsTwo shapes, TD’s option, defaulting to promotion: the lowest net beaten finalist fills the fourth seat — he still has to win two matches, so nothing is handed to him — or all three play points for nine and the two leaders play a match on the back.Bracket setup
✓Finalists keep their side games, but sit out the day betThe champions’ foursome plays the ball game and Irish Rumble as normal on Sunday. They are out of the day-2 stroke bet — they are playing a match, not a card against the field.Bracket setup
✓The day 2 foursome is derived, never setThe TD cannot know the four winners when he builds Sunday’s groups, so the groups step has to reserve that foursome rather than have him rebuild it on the morning.Bracket setup
✓The bracket is four matches per groupTwo semis on the front, final and 3rd place together on the back.Bracket setup
✓No game gets a second rules screenThe mode picker expands in place — Custom per-hole opens its 18-cell editor under the radio. Rules first, money last, one Save, for all four games.Rumble setup
✓A threesome borrows a 4th from the fieldBuilt already: automatic, only when another group has four, donors rotate across every other golfer, and the donor’s own net on his own tee is what counts. Called Borrowed 4th everywhere; a group waiting on a donor shows a provisional total on three balls.Rumble setup
✓The TD names the ball game, and the app never asks the colourCommonly Pink Ball; one group buys red and renames it, another runs Devil Ball or Beer Ball. Free text, 16 characters — the iPhone 13 mini cap from the Cup work — carried to the tab, the carrier badge and the chat string.Ball setup
✓The ball game’s rules live on its setup screenOne ball, no replacements, last group holding it wins, low net breaks a tie at 18. Live states none of them anywhere.Ball setup
✓Side-game entry is priced at signupA flat fee per game, taken with the entry, so a golfer’s games are known before round one and the side-game step never reopens.Step 1

Money 10 decided

✓The bracket pot is carved out of the championship poolNo separate entry — a percentage comes off the top for the champions’ foursome. 4th breaks even (his entry back) and the other three split the rest. Nobody stakes a game he can only reach by winning his group.Bracket setup
✓The final round’s day bet pays one or two placesThe bracket finalists are out of it and the championship money winners are disqualified from it, so a 16-golfer field leaves ten eligible — fewer on a smaller field. Three places would pay most of the men who entered.Day bet board
✓The day bet must not out-pay the championship’s last placeWinning 36-hole money disqualifies a golfer from the day bet, so if the last paying place in the championship paid less than day bet 1st, finishing in the money would cost him money. Checked at the payout step: last paying championship place ≥ day bet 1st.Day bet board
✓Match play and stroke play money are independentA prize for the bracket champion, a prize for the stroke play winner, and it can be the same man. Nothing disqualifies either from the other — unlike the day bet, where 36-hole money takes you out.Bracket setup
✓Every paid place names its recipient1st — golfer or 1st — foursome, splits to $20.00 each. The same 1st / 2nd / 3rd table means three different things across the four games.Rumble · Bracket
✓The pool line names its scope and its count$10 × 4 in this foursome = $40 against $10 × 8 in the field = $80, in cards that otherwise look identical.Bracket setup
✓Tied places split the money for the places they occupyA T2 shares 2nd and 3rd — $16 each, not $20 each. Indexing prize by position overpays the pool.Championship board
✓A levelled group splits its place three waysThe borrowed 4th is not a person and cannot be paid, so three winning golfers take $23.33 each where four would take $17.50 — accepted, since the borrowed score comes from a stranger they did not pick.Rumble setup
✓Ties split, and there are no tiebreakersPlenty of bets run on a given day, so a tie can be no action. Tied foursomes or golfers split the place — arbitrary countbacks decide real money on a rule nobody agreed to. The split has to be stated, not silent.Rumble setup
✓Money is a projection until the round closesMuted italic all round, with one line under the table. Live shows $48 in green against a golfer thru 1.Championship board
✓The day bet DQ follows the tournament’s handicap settingNet event, net winners; gross event, gross. Eligibility is not knowable until the last round ends, so the Round 2 board shows 36-hole money leaders in italics rather than hiding them.Flow map · 5

Scope — who sets what 4 decided

✓The TD sets tournament games onlyIrish Rumble, the ball game, Mini Singles Bracket, the day bet. In-foursome side games are the foursome’s to set — skins, Nassau, rabbit, survivor, sixes — configured exactly as in a casual round. The TD never sees them.Play map · 16a
✓Foursome side bets read the tournament’s score entryNo second card, and none of the specialised entry surfaces those games have casually. A rabbit or survivor running inside a tournament foursome is read from the leaderboard, not from its own screen.Play map · 16a
✓Foursome side bets settle in the group, not on the settlement tabOnly the TD’s tournament-scope games appear in settlement totals. A foursome’s own bets are paid out inside the foursome — the TD never set them, never collected for them, and should not be accountable for money he did not handle.Settlement
✓Spots are the exception — they need score entryA spot is a fact about a hole that no score implies, so it cannot be derived later. The casual control carries over unchanged: a − / + stepper per golfer per hole. A count, never a type — no greenie or sandie taxonomy, least of all in a tournament — and no stepper at all unless this group chose spots — and none on a golfer who opted out. A tournament foursome already carrying the ball game, Irish Rumble and a bracket rarely adds one, so no-steppers is the default card.Score entry

Play surfaces 10 decided

✓The ball game ranks by survival, not by scoreThe last group still holding it wins, so groups that are out rank by the hole the ball died on, latest first. Net appears on every row but decides nothing unless two groups finish 18 with it alive.Ball board
✓Full names on summary rows, short names on cardsA hole-by-hole card has about forty pixels for a name. The short name is five characters and defaults to initials until the TD sets one; the borrowed ball is Borrowed 4th on the summary and 4th on the card.Rumble board
✓Leaderboards carry stroke detail, not headlinesThe expanded row opens all 18 — par, stroke index, gross with a dot per stroke received, and the net being ranked — instead of a two-column fragment.Championship board
✓Four round columns, then the strip scrolls to the newestSix rounds will not fit beside a name and a total. The round columns are their own horizontal strip — every row scrolls with the header, and it opens scrolled to the most recent round, which is what anyone opening the board mid-tournament came for.Championship board
✓Every round gets a column, and the tournament can count the best NAll rounds show. A longer outing counts best 3 of 4 or best 4 of 6 so a golfer can sit a round out; the dropped round is struck through, not hidden, and moves as scores land.Championship board
✓The ball game is named fresh every tournamentNo default, no memory of last week. Save does not fire until it has a name.Ball setup
✓A Stableford card shows gross and points, not per-hole netThe stroke allocation is on the card, so a golfer can take his own 18-hole net off the gross. One method, one board — a Stableford tournament does not also draw a net stroke board.Championship board
✓Rows still on the course are markedA net score without its holes is not comparable, and a leader thru 11 must not read like a finished one.Championship board
✓The ball game ranks by the ballLast to lose it wins, so status is the ranking — still alive, then lost latest — and score is the tiebreak only among groups that finish 18 with it.Play map · 9
✓Nothing is disabled without saying whyInherited from the Cup work, and it applies to Save Configuration, the next-hole button, and every balance check in these screens.Play map · 13
