Scoring, Individual

One step for both methods. Tap the segment to switch — it is the same answer given on step 1, editable here rather than by walking back.

The max is a rule, not a setting

Live, stroke play ships as two formats: with and without a net double bogey max. That is one setting wearing a format's clothes, and the field cannot tell from the leaderboard which one it entered. Here the max is always on and stated as a rule with a worked example, so a golfer who picks up on a hole knows exactly what lands on his card.

The cappar + 2 + strokes received on that hole. Par 4, one stroke → 7. Par 3, no stroke → 5.
What it protectsA net total, not a hole result. Group bets and skins still read the real gross.
Versus StablefordThe max keeps a blow-up hole at a ceiling; Stableford stops scoring it at all. Different mechanisms, and both are drawn here so the difference is visible before the field tees off.

Stableford scale — the casual screen, reused

Casual rounds already ask this question on their own setup screen, so the tournament uses that screen rather than a second one. The block drawn below is the read-back on this step; the scale itself is set where casual sets it. One screen, one scale, two entry points.

Standard and modified are both offered and stored on the tournament — never a global preference, because a club's Sunday game and its member-guest use different scales. The table renders under the choice rather than in help text, since the scale is the whole answer.

No strokes off

Strokes off the low index does not appear on this type. It is a Cup mechanism. Individual play is net or gross with an allowance percentage, and the allowance applies to the whole field.

Rounds that count

Only asked when the tournament has more than two rounds. A one or two-round event counts everything and the block does not render — there is no interesting answer to give.

Above that, the TD sets it: all four, best 3 of 4, best 4 of 6. A multi-day 36-hole outing is the case that needs it — a golfer sits a round out and stays in the championship instead of conceding it on Friday.

The board reads this directly: a column per round, the dropped one struck through so a golfer can see what he is throwing away, recalculated as scores land. A round in progress does not compete for a counted place, or four holes at level par would knock out a finished 73. Drawn on the championship board.

Flights

Off by default. When on, the field splits by index range and every board — gross, net, Stableford — is drawn per flight. Left as a toggle on this step because it changes what the leaderboard is, and asking it later means redrawing results a golfer has already seen.

Still open

  • Flights are deferred — not implemented. Intent when they land is an even split of the field rather than index ranges. Nothing else on this step depends on them.
  • Whether a modified-Stableford tournament should still show a net stroke-play board alongside the points board. Currently not drawn — one method, one board.
