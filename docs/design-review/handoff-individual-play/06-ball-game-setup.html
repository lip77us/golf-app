The ball game, setup

The colour picker is gone. The game is commonly Pink Ball; one group buys red balls and calls it Red Ball; another runs Devil Ball, or Beer Ball because the ball had a stein printed on it. The app does not need to know the colour — it needs to know what the group calls the game.

So this screen asks one question, in a text field, and that answer is the game’s name on every surface: tab, badge, lost-ball control, chat string, settlement line.

Sixteen characters

The same cap the Cup work derived from an iPhone 13 mini — the narrowest screen we support. Sixteen characters fits a leaderboard tab without truncating and a carrier badge on a score-entry row that already holds a name and a CH chip.

FitsPink Ball · Devil Ball · Beer Ball · Rainbow Ball (12)
Cap16 characters, counted live. The field stops accepting at the cap rather than truncating later.
DefaultNone. The field starts empty every tournament — no memory, no carry-over from last week.

The four suggestions are one tap each — shortcuts, not a list to choose from. Anything that fits is valid.

Set fresh every tournament. No default and no memory of what this group called it last time: the ball in the bag this week is the one being named, and Save does not fire until it has a name.

A fresh ball every day

The ball game is re-run each round. A new ball goes in the bag, a new entry is taken and a new group wins — a group that loses it on the 3rd on Saturday is back in it on Sunday. The name carries across; the pot does not.

Rules on the screen that sets them

Live shows a colour field and no rules at all. The four that decide the money are stated where the money is: one ball, no replacements, last group to lose it wins, low net breaks a tie between groups that finish 18 with it alive.

What the preview is for

Three lines showing the name in the places it has to survive — a tab, a badge on a crowded row, and a sentence. Type something long and you see it press against the cap before you save, rather than finding it clipped on the first tee.
