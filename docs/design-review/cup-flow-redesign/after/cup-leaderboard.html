Cup leaderboard — one Cup Detail tab

Live testing on an eight-group cup reworked this screen after handoff. What is drawn here is what shipped; the mock is redrawn to match so the rest of the pack stays true.

Navigation

  • Overview, Details and My Foursome collapsed into one Cup Detail tab. Overview showed less data in more space, and My Foursome only duplicated a group card. The two-panel scoreboard sits on top, then a card per group.
  • Your group floats to the top — that is what replaces My Foursome — and the rest follow in tee-time order. The header carries the start time, so Group 4 (my group) · 8:30 AM tells a player both where they are and when they go.

Group cards

  • Full names once, initials in the rows. Each side is named in full at the top of the card; the four segment rows use BK / AP vs RS / RW to stay compact at 390px.
  • A solo side’s Phantom is named in the header, real golfer first — “Charlie Wicke & Phantom” — so the lone P in the Fourball row is explained where it appears.
  • Cards no longer drill in. A Triple Cup has no per-foursome bet, so the standalone per-group screen carried nothing the card does not already show.

The bar and the count

  • The marker is the even line, not the clinch line. It sits dead centre, on the divider between the two team blocks: whichever fill crosses it is ahead. Putting it at to-win meant a tied cup did not line up with the divider. “16½ to win” stays in the text underneath.
  • “Points decided,” not “points played.” A cup point is not earned until its match is decided, however many holes have been played.

Open

  • Segment rows assume four per group. Formats with 2 or 6 need the list to reflow, not scroll.
  • Live tee-time editing is a new surface the mocks did not cover — drawn separately in Edit tee times.
