What I would change, in order

  1. Ask the tournament type first. It determines whether the draft phase, cup design and match selection exist. Asked second, the wizard cannot honestly tell you how long it is.
  2. Stop asking for the group count. Derive it from round setup. This is the single change that removes a guess from the middle of the flow — done in Games by round.
  3. Relabel the wizard end. Review should read Save & continue to draft, and the created screen should say what remains: draft, then one round setup per round.
  4. Collapse round setup from five screens to one group builder. Matches, tees and tee time are three screens describing one group; a captain building four groups walks twelve screens to do one job. Tee time defaults to previous + 10.
  5. Give players one owner. They are named at 15, drafted at 23, and selected again at 24. The first is a count, the second is entitlement, the third is assignment — worth making that distinction visible rather than repeating a list.
  6. Skip the secondary-game step when the format is exclusive. Triple Cup forbids it, so asking is a dead end.

Where the four families diverge

What varies between tournaments is how many golfers share a score, and the rest follows from it — so the families are named for the scoring unit rather than for a game. Skins, rabbit and Nassau are group bets rather than types, and they need individual gross, which is why the last row is the one that decides them.

Two consequences. The group builder is shared work — all four families walk it, which makes it the highest-leverage screen in the flow and the one currently spread across the most screens. And the one-ball formats need something the app does not have: alternate shot, Chapman and scramble put a single card in for two or four golfers. Best ball, shamble and bramble keep individual cards, so they are the cheap half.
