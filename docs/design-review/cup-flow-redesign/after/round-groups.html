Groups for this round

Shipped step 27 is where the group count finally becomes real — six steps after the wizard asked for it. It lists groups by number and little else, which was survivable when three separate screens held the details. With one group builder behind it, this screen has to show what the builder wrote.

WasGroup rows, a sitting out list, and Start Round enabled regardless of state.
NowEach row carries composition, tee time and tees — the three things that go wrong on the first tee.
StartEnabled once one group exists. Unassigned golfers are named with an Assign link rather than held against the round.

Decisions

  • This is the hub of step 7, not a summary. Every group is reached from here, added from here, and checked from here. It is the screen a captain stands on while the group builder opens and closes over it.
  • Composition is repeated per row because a legal group and a full group are different states. Group 3 is a legal 2 v 2 like the rest; a 1 v 2 would be legal too, just smaller.
  • Unassigned golfers are listed by name, not counted. A number tells a captain there is a problem; the names tell him exactly who is not teeing off.
  • Start Round does not block on a sit-out. The builder cannot seat an illegal group in the first place, and a golfer left out is usually a deliberate rest rather than a mistake — so the button is live once one group exists, and the golfers not in one are named above it. Blocking here would stop a legitimate round on a fact the captain already knows.
  • Tee times read as a sequence down the list, so an interval mistake is visible as a gap rather than found by arithmetic.
  • No bets on this list. A wager inside a foursome is theirs, started through their own entry — the same mechanism multi-group skins uses. This screen carries only what the TD sets: golfers, tees, tee time, start format.

Still open

  • Reordering groups. Tee times imply the order, so dragging a group ought to renumber and re-time it — which is a bigger interaction than it looks.
