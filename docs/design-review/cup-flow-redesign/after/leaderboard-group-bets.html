Group bets on the leaderboard

Skins, Nassau and rabbit are the three games the shipped build treats as tournament types, each with its own setup flow. They are none of those things: they are wagers between the four people in one group, and every number they need is already in the gross scores the tournament collects. So they are a leaderboard tab, not a flow.

WasSeparate setups and separate leaderboards per game — Skins, Nassau, Rabbit.
NowSet on the group in the group builder, settled here from the same card.
CostNo new score entry. One tab and the settlement rules.

Decisions

  • Grouped by group, not by game. A player has one foursome and cares about its money. Sorting by game would scatter a single group across three sections.
  • The tab says where its numbers come from. Anything settling money has to be auditable, and the honest answer is short: gross, as entered on the card.
  • Skins shows the hole strip, because a skins argument is always about a specific hole. Carryovers are marked in place rather than summarised.
  • Nassau reads front, back, total, which is the whole game — three numbers, not a running balance.
  • Rabbit shows who holds it, since a rabbit loose or held is the only state that matters until 18.
  • Groups without a bet are not hidden; the empty row keeps the tab honest about scope and offers the way to add one.

Still open

  • Gross or net skins. Gross is drawn here; net needs the stroke dots from Details repeated, which crowds the strip.
  • Press. The Nassau screens support it and this tab does not show it yet — a pressed match is a fourth column, or a second row.
  • Whether to settle in currency at all, or only report the result. Naming amounts makes the app a ledger.
