Cup hub — live card

The shipped card lists six rows of equal weight, mixes navigation with round-scoped setup, prints the same date twice, uses two trophy icons for two different destinations, and gives the loudest colour on the screen to delete. It also shows no score — the one thing a player opening the app during a cup wants.

What changed

  • The card carries state. Live team tally, the clinch bar, and a line saying which round is running and how far along. You can answer “are we up?” without a tap.
  • One primary action. During a live round that is Enter Scores. Between rounds it becomes Set Up Round 2; after the last round, View Result. The card always proposes the next thing.
  • Navigation vs. setup, separated. Leaderboard and Draft sit as two quiet buttons; the three round-scoped actions collapse into Round 1 setup, which is where a TD looks and a player never does.
  • Delete leaves the surface. It moves into the ⋮ menu with the other card-level actions. A destructive action should not be the brightest pixel on a screen.
  • Leaderboard and Scoreboard merged. Same trophy, adjacent rows, unclear difference — they are one destination with the view tabs from the leaderboard screen.
  • The date prints once. Course and date belong to the round line, not the tournament title.

Still open

  • A multi-round cup needs the bar to show round boundaries, not just total points.
  • Whether non-TD players should see the setup disclosure at all, or a read-only summary.
