Type first

The shipped wizard asks the tournament type second, and calls itself “4 of 4” regardless. But the type decides whether teams, a draft and match selection exist at all — so until it is answered, the wizard cannot say how long it is, and the answer it gives is wrong for every type.

The type is a scoring unit

Cup, stroke and Stableford are not four options on one list — they answer different questions. What actually varies between tournaments is how many golfers share a score, and everything downstream follows from it: who enters a number, what a leaderboard row is, and whether a group bet can exist at all.

SideCup play. Drafted teams, match segments, points to a side. Scores still land per golfer inside the segments.
GolferIndividual. Stroke play and Stableford. One score per golfer against the field.
PairTwo-man team. Chapman and alternate shot put one ball in the hole; best ball keeps two cards.
GroupFour-man team. Scramble puts one ball in the hole. Shamble and bramble keep individual cards after the drive.

The consequence strip under the format list states the three things that follow, in the app’s own words, before any of them cost a screen. Group bets fall out of it rather than being special-cased: Skins and Nassau need individual gross, so they are available under best ball and shamble and unavailable under alternate shot and scramble — and no rule has to name a format.

Recommendation 1 — ask type first

  • Type and format are the first and only question on step 1. Everything downstream is a consequence of them.
  • The step list is derived and shown immediately. A cup on Triple Cup has six steps left; stroke play has six of a different shape; Stableford has seven. Nobody is told “4 of 4” and then handed eight more screens.
  • A cup can mix formats across rounds — Triple Cup one day, Singles the next. Triple Cup is exclusive because its segments own all 18 holes, so nothing else can run alongside it.
  • Scoring unit is a property of the format, not the type. A two-man team can be one ball or two; a four-man team can be one card or four. That is why the strip reads off the format and not the type above it.
  • The two numbers agree: the header counts this step plus what remains, and the panel is titled for what is left.
  • Because the list is real, the progress bar can be honest about position.

Recommendation 6 — drop steps that have no answer

Triple Cup is exclusive: no other game can run on the round. The shipped wizard still presents a Side game step, which cannot be answered. Here it is struck from the plan the moment an exclusive format is chosen, with the reason attached — so the removal reads as the format working, not as a missing screen.

Recommendation 5 — players get one owner

Players are currently named in three places, meaning three different things. Naming the meanings is what removes the duplication:

EntitlementWho is in the tournament. One owner — the draft for a cup, the field for stroke or Stableford. Round details no longer asks for a player count; it is the size of the roster.
SideWhich team a golfer belongs to. Set once, in the draft.
AssignmentWhich group and which round. Set in the group builder, and changeable on the morning without touching either of the above.

Still open

  • Skins, rabbit and Nassau are not tournament types — and not tournament side games either. They are group bets, set on the group rather than the field, and they are only a leaderboard addition: the gross scores the tournament already collects are all they need. A tournament side game is a different thing at a different scope — Irish Rumble or Pink Ball, played by every foursome and scored across the field — which is what this step collects.
  • Whether one-ball formats are worth building now. Alternate shot, Chapman and scramble all need score entry to accept a single card for two or four golfers, which the app currently has no shape for. Best ball, shamble and bramble need none of that — they are individual cards with a different aggregation, so they are the cheap half of this list.
  • Whether the step list should be tappable as a jump-to — useful for editing an existing tournament, confusing during first setup.
