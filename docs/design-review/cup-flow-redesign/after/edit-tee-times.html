New surface

Edit tee times — live

The tee-time pencil only existed in initial setup, and saving there means re-running setup. On a running cup a starter change — a group goes off ten minutes late — had nowhere to go.

This adds Edit tee times under Round setup: the round’s groups in tee-time order, tap a time, save on the spot.

Behaviour

  • Each change saves immediately. No save bar, no confirm step — a starter is standing on the tee, not filling in a form.
  • Then it offers the cascade. Moving one group almost always moves the ones behind it, so after a change the sheet offers to shift every later group by the same amount. The prompt states the amount and lists the groups it would touch, old time struck through.
  • Shifting is opt-in, once. “Only this group” dismisses it; the sheet does not come back for that edit.
  • Groups that have already started keep their time as a plain value and sit out of the shift — their time is a fact, not a plan.

Open

  • Whether a shift should notify the affected players, and in what words.
  • Interval-based rounds (shotgun off) already have an interval in group setup — a later pass could offer “re-space all” instead of a per-group nudge.
