Decider
PaulDave
Low score of the two takes it. Tied → they carry to the next hole.

As soon as a Survivor is decided a fresh one starts on the very next hole, all three back in — so a full round can play out up to nine of them.

The last hole has no room to eliminate and then decide, so it settles what’s standing: with all three in, the low ball takes it and any tie for low is no blood; with two left, a tie splits the eliminated player’s entry.
