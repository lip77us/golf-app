How to play Two‑man Best Ball

Two partners, two balls, and the better net counts on every hole. The member-guest standard.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Both partners play every shot of their own ball. The lower net of the two is the team score for the hole. Add the 18 and the lowest total in the field wins. Each partner plays off 85% of his course handicap.

The rules

1
Own ball
Both partners play their own ball from tee to hole, with their own strokes.
2
Better net
On every hole the lower net of the two is the pair’s score.
3
Lowest pair wins
Add the 18 hole scores. The lowest total in the field wins.

One partner can carry a hole the other has lost. Once your partner’s net can’t be beaten, you can pick up.

Handicap

Best ball is the only pairs format where each golfer keeps his own handicap: 85% of his course handicap, on his own ball. Mike, a 4, gets 3 strokes. Tom, a 19, gets 16.

The same two golfers get three times the strokes in alternate shot that they get in a scramble, purely from the format. Every figure is worked from course handicap, rounded once on the total, half up.

An odd field

Every golfer needs a partner. Best ball is the one pairs format where a team may play three: the best net of the three counts. The organiser can offer that, add a golfer, or take one out.

Drives

Both partners drive on every hole, so there are no drive requirements in best ball.

The money

Entry and the pool
Every golfer pays the same entry into one pool. The organiser sets how many places pay, from one to four, and the split.
Paid to the pair
Each place pays the pair, and the prize divides two ways.
Ties
Tied pairs split the places they cover.

Questions people ask

How does two-man best ball work?

Both partners play their own ball on every hole, and the better net score of the two counts for the team. The lowest team total wins.

What handicap allowance is used in two-man best ball?

85% of each golfer’s course handicap, on his own ball.

What is the difference between best ball and fourball?

Fourball is the same scoring played as a match, two pairs against each other hole by hole. Best ball usually means a team total over 18 holes against the field.

Do both partners have to finish every hole?

No. Once one partner’s net can’t be beaten by the other, he can pick up.

Can a team have three golfers?

In best ball, yes, when the field is odd and the organiser allows it. The best net of the three counts.
