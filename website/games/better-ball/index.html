How to play Better Ball

A team game for a field of foursomes. Every golfer plays his own ball, and the group’s best nets count on every hole. Count one, two, three or all four.

THE SHORT ANSWER

The organiser picks how many balls count, and that number stays the same for all 18 holes. Add the counting nets on every hole. The group with the lowest total to par wins the pool.

The rules

1
Own ball
Every golfer plays his own ball all the way in. Each group of four is a team.
2
Best nets count
On every hole, the group’s best N nets count. N is chosen before the round and never moves.
3
Lowest group wins
Add the counting nets against par for 18 holes. The lowest group in the field wins.

When golfers tie for a counting place it makes no difference which of them counts: the score is the same.

Picking the count

The count is the game. The fewer balls count, the more one good golfer carries the group. The name follows the count until the organiser types his own.

Handicap and the cap

Each golfer plays off a share of his own course handicap. The allowance follows the count by default, from the table above. The organiser can set his own number, and the app stops suggesting once he does.

The net double bogey cap is always on. Every score is capped before the best nets are picked, so one disaster can’t sink a hole where all four count.

Groups of three

If some groups have four and others three, each threesome plays with a borrowed 4th. On every hole the fourth score comes from a golfer in another group, in a fixed rotation through the field, at his own net. The threesome doesn’t pick who.

The money

One pool
Every golfer pays the same entry into one pool for the field.
Paid to the group
Each paid place goes to a group, and the prize splits among its real golfers. The borrowed 4th is not paid.
Ties
Tied groups split the places they cover. There is no countback.
One group game a round
A round runs Better Ball, Irish Rumble or 40 Balls, never two. They rank the same groups off the same cards.

Better Ball or Irish Rumble?

Both count a group’s best nets. In Irish Rumble the count climbs through the round, and the group plays around that. In Better Ball it never moves, so the group knows on the first tee exactly how many balls it needs all day.

Questions people ask

How does Better Ball work in golf?

Each group of four is a team. Everyone plays his own ball, and the group’s best nets count on every hole. The lowest group total wins.

How many balls count in Better Ball?

One, two, three or four, chosen before the round. The same number counts on all 18 holes. Best 2 of 4 is the default.

What handicap allowance does Better Ball use?

By default it follows the count: 75% of course handicap at one ball, 85% at two, 95% at three and 100% when all four count.

What is aggregate in golf?

Every golfer’s net counts on every hole. It is Better Ball with all four counting.

What happens with a threesome?

It plays with a borrowed 4th: on every hole a golfer from another group lends his net score, in a fixed rotation through the field.
