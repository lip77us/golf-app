How to play a Scramble

Everyone hits, the team picks the best shot, and everyone plays the next one from there. One ball, one score a hole, and the lowest net total wins.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Every golfer tees off, the team picks the best drive, and everyone plays from that spot, again and again until the ball is holed. One team score a hole. The team handicap is a slice of each golfer’s handicap, lowest first: 25, 20, 15 and 10% for four.

The rules

1
Everyone hits
All the golfers on the team hit from the tee.
2
Pick the best
The team chooses the ball it likes best. The others are picked up.
3
Play from there
Everyone places a ball within a club length of that spot, no nearer the hole, and hits again. On the green, putt from the marked spot.

Repeat until the ball is in. The team writes down one score, and that score is the team’s for the hole.

The team handicap

A scramble is far easier than one golfer’s ball, so the team gets only a slice of each golfer’s course handicap. The biggest slice goes to the lowest handicap, which is why the team is always listed low to high.

Round once, on the total
Add the four slices at full precision and round at the end. Rounding each one first turns 6.15 into 7.
Half rounds up
7.50 is 8.
Whole strokes
The team plays off a whole number. That makes ties normal, so settle how they split before you start.

Two-man example: a 4 and a 19 is 1.40 + 2.85 = 4.25, so the pair plays off 4. It is all worked from course handicap, off the tees you are playing.

Drive requirements

Without a rule, the long hitter drives every hole. Most events require a number of drives from each golfer.

None. Take any drive you like.
Per nine. Each golfer’s drives are counted on the front and the back separately. The front doesn’t carry to the back.
Per eighteen. One count for the round. The most slack.
Alternating pairs. Four-man only. Two golfers drive on each hole in a rota the team sets on the 1st tee, fixed for 18 holes.

Falling short costs nothing unless the organiser says so. The optional penalty is two strokes per missing drive, added to the team’s gross at the end of the round.

A team of three

A four-man team that is one short plays with a phantom 4th, handicapped at the average of the three real golfers. Nobody from another team is borrowed: his good shots would cost his own team.

The phantom can’t be paid, so a team of three splits its prize three ways. In a two-man scramble every golfer needs a partner: there are no teams of three.

Two v two in one group

One foursome can play it too: two pairs, a scramble each, as a match. The lower team score wins the hole and a tie halves it. Each pair plays off 35% of its low handicap plus 15% of its high, and the difference between the two pairs is the strokes.

The drive requirement works here too. Set it at the start: none, per nine or per eighteen, and how many drives each golfer owes. Each hole, tap whose drive the pair took, and the card shows who still owes one and warns before a pair runs out of holes. It is a record, not a penalty: falling short costs nothing, and the match is decided on the score alone.

The money

Entry and the pool
Every golfer pays the same entry into one pool. The organiser sets how many places pay, from one to four, and the split.
Paid to the team
Each place pays the team, and the prize divides among its golfers.
Ties
Tied teams split the places they cover.

Questions people ask

How does a scramble work in golf?

Everyone on the team hits, the team picks the best shot, and everyone plays the next shot from there. One team score a hole, lowest total wins.

How do you work out a scramble team handicap?

For a four-man team, 25% of the lowest course handicap, 20% of the next, 15% and 10%, added up and rounded once. For a two-man team, 35% of the low plus 15% of the high.

Do you have to use everyone’s drives?

Only if the event sets a drive requirement, usually a number of drives from each golfer per nine or per eighteen.

What happens if a team only has three?

It plays with a phantom 4th, handicapped at the average of the three. Nobody from another team is borrowed.

Can one foursome play a scramble?

Yes, as two pairs. Each pair plays a scramble and the lower team score wins the hole, as a match. Each pair plays off 35% of its low handicap plus 15% of its high. The drive requirement can be set for the match too.

What is the difference between a scramble and a shamble?

In a scramble the team plays the best shot every time. In a shamble it picks the best drive, then every golfer plays his own ball in.
