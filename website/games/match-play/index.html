How to play Match Play

You against one other golfer, a hole at a time. Win more holes than he does and you win the match, whatever the scores add up to.

THE SHORT ANSWER

The lower score on a hole wins it, and a tie halves it. The match is counted in holes: 2 up, all square, dormie. It ends as soon as one golfer leads by more holes than are left, which is how a match finishes 3&2.

The rules

1
Win the hole
The lower score on a hole wins it. Equal scores halve it, and nobody wins it.
2
Keep the count
The match is told in holes: 1 up, 2 down, all square. Strokes only matter on the hole they are played.
3
Close it out
Once you lead by more holes than are left, it is over. 3&2 means three up with two to play.

A 9 on one hole only loses that hole. That is the whole charm of match play: you can have a terrible hole and still win the match.

Reading the match

All square. Level. Nobody is up.
2 up. You have won two more holes than he has.
Dormie. Up by as many holes as are left. You can’t lose, but he can still halve the match.
3&2. Won three up with two holes to play. The last two aren’t played for the match.
1 up. Won by one hole, on the last.
Halved. Level after 18. Play extra holes only if you agreed to before you started.

Handicap strokes

A match is played off the low man. The better golfer plays to scratch, and the other gets the difference in course handicaps, one stroke on each of the hardest holes by stroke index.

Concessions

In match play you can concede a putt, a hole or the whole match. A conceded putt counts as holed with your next stroke, so enter the score it would have made. A conceded hole is a hole lost. Once given, a concession can’t be taken back.

The bet

One bet on the match. The winner collects the stake, and a halved match is a push.

Want three bets in one round? Front nine, back nine and the 18 is a Nassau. Two on two, each on his own ball, is Fourball.

Questions people ask

How does match play work in golf?

Two golfers play each hole, and the lower score wins it. A tie halves it. Whoever wins more holes wins the match.

What does dormie mean?

Up by as many holes as are left. The leader can no longer lose the match, but it can still be halved.

What does 3&2 mean in golf?

The match was won three up with two holes left to play. The match ended on the 16th green.

How many strokes do I get in match play?

Usually the difference between your course handicap and your opponent’s, one stroke on each of the hardest holes by stroke index.

What happens if a match is all square after 18?

It is halved and the bet is a push, unless you agreed before the round to play extra holes.

Can two golfers in a group play a match while the group plays something else?

Yes. In Halved, a Singles Match can run alongside a team Nassau or another game on the same scores.
