How to play Scotch

Alternate shot with a softer start. Both partners drive, the pair picks one, and from there they take turns.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Both tee off and the pair picks one drive. The partner whose drive was not taken plays the second shot, then they alternate until it’s holed. The pair plays off 60% of the low handicap plus 40% of the high.

The rules

1
Both drive
Both partners tee off on every hole.
2
Pick one
The pair picks the drive it likes. The other ball is picked up.
3
Then alternate
The partner whose drive wasn’t taken plays the second shot, and they alternate from there until it’s holed.

In Britain the same format is called greensomes. Some clubs use the name Scotch for other games, so say which one you mean on the 1st tee.

Drive requirements

As in a scramble, the organiser can require a number of drives from each partner, per nine or per eighteen. One each per nine is the usual rule, and with two golfers it leaves plenty of slack.

With or without a rule, the pick happens on every hole, because it decides who hits next.

Handicap

The pair plays off 60% of the lower course handicap plus 40% of the higher. Chapman uses the same figure: both are two drives, then one ball.

The same two golfers get three times the strokes in alternate shot that they get in a scramble, purely from the format. Every figure is worked from course handicap, rounded once on the total, half up.

The money

Entry and the pool
Every golfer pays the same entry into one pool. The organiser sets how many places pay, from one to four, and the split.
Paid to the pair
Each place pays the pair, and the prize divides two ways.
Ties
Tied pairs split the places they cover.

Questions people ask

How does Scotch work in golf?

Both partners tee off, the pair picks one drive, and the partner whose drive wasn’t taken plays the second shot. They alternate shots from there until the ball is holed.

What is the difference between Scotch and alternate shot?

In alternate shot only one partner tees off on each hole. In Scotch both drive and the pair picks one, then they alternate.

Is Scotch the same as greensomes?

Yes, this format is called greensomes in Britain. Scotch is used for other games at some clubs, so agree the rules first.

What handicap do you get in Scotch?

60% of the lower course handicap plus 40% of the higher, rounded once.

What is the difference between Scotch and Chapman?

In Chapman both partners also hit a second shot, with each other’s ball, before the pair picks one.
