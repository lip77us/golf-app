How to play a Shamble

Half scramble, half better ball. The team picks the best drive, then every golfer plays his own ball into the hole. The best two scores count, or set your own count.

THE SHORT ANSWER

The rules

1
Pick a drive
All four tee off. The team chooses one drive, and the other three are picked up.
2
Own ball in
Everyone places a ball within a club length of that drive, no nearer the hole, and plays it out with his own strokes.
3
Best nets count
The best nets on the hole make the team score. Lowest total for 18 holes wins.

Everyone starts each hole from a good drive, which takes the worst holes out of the round, and everyone still has to finish the job.

The ball count

How many scores count is set before the round. Four ways to set it:

ModeHow many count
FixedThe same on every hole. Best 2 of 4 is the default.
Escalating1 on holes 1–6, 2 on 7–12, 3 on 13–18
Par-based3 on a par 3, 2 on a par 4, 1 on a par 5
Per holeThe organiser sets each of the 18

A hole set to all four is allowed but flagged: nobody can drop a bad one there. The count is locked once the first score is in.

Handicap

Every golfer keeps his own strokes, at one percentage of his course handicap that follows how many balls count on average:

Average countAllowance
1 ball75%
2 balls85%
3 balls95%

Fixed best 2 and escalating both average two balls, so both play at 85%. The organiser can set one flat figure instead.

Drive requirements

The drive is chosen, so most events require a number of drives from each golfer, per nine or per eighteen. Per nine is two windows: the front doesn’t carry to the back.

Falling short costs nothing unless the organiser says so. The optional penalty is two strokes per missing drive, added to the team’s total at the end.

A team of three

A team one short plays with a phantom 4th, handicapped at the average of the three real golfers. Its ball is played and scored like any other, and it can count. Nobody from another team is borrowed. The phantom can’t be paid, so the team splits its prize three ways.

The money

Entry and the pool
Every golfer pays the same entry into one pool. The organiser sets how many places pay and the split.
Paid to the team
Each place pays the team, and the prize divides among its golfers.
Ties
Tied teams split the places they cover.

Questions people ask

How does a shamble work in golf?

All four golfers tee off and the team picks one drive. Everyone plays his own ball in from that spot, and the best scores on the hole count for the team.

What is the difference between a shamble and a scramble?

A scramble plays the best shot every time. A shamble only picks the drive, then every golfer plays his own ball into the hole.

How many scores count in a shamble?

The best two of four is the most common. Some events count one early and more late, or count by par.

What handicap allowance does a shamble use?

A share of each golfer’s own course handicap that follows the count: 75% at one ball, 85% at two, 95% at three.

Do drive requirements apply in a shamble?

They often do, because the drive is chosen. A typical rule is a number of drives from each golfer per nine.
