How to play Fourball

Two against two, every golfer on his own ball. The better score on each side is the side’s score for the hole, and the hole goes to the lower one.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Each side takes its better net on every hole. The lower one wins the hole, and a tie halves it. It is match play, so the match ends when one side is up by more holes than are left. The Ryder Cup’s fourballs are this game.

The rules

1
Own ball
Every golfer plays his own ball all the way in, with his own strokes.
2
Better ball
On each side, the lower net is the side’s score for the hole. The other one doesn’t count.
3
Holes won
The lower side score wins the hole and a tie halves it. Most holes won wins the match.

The teams are fixed for all 18 holes. Because only the better ball counts, a golfer can pick up once his partner has the hole covered.

Handicap

Halved’s default is strokes off the low: the lowest of the four plays to scratch, and everyone else gets the difference on his own ball.

Strokes go on the hardest holes by stroke index. Net with an allowance, and gross, are the other two options.

There is no net double bogey cap in Fourball. Each hole is decided on the real net score, and a cap could change who won it.

The bet

One bet on the match, per golfer. Each winner collects the stake from a loser, and a halved match is a push. Halved’s Fourball has no presses.

For three bets in one round, play a Nassau in teams of two instead.

Playing it well

Get one ball in play
When your partner is in trouble, your job is the fairway. Two aggressive balls is how sides lose holes with a 6.
Watch the stroke holes
A golfer with a stroke on a hard hole is often the side’s best ball there. Know before you tee off.
Go second when you can
Seeing your partner’s ball safe first lets you attack the flag.

Questions people ask

How does fourball work in golf?

Two sides of two, every golfer on his own ball. On each hole the better net score on each side counts, and the lower one wins the hole. It is match play.

What is the difference between fourball and foursomes?

In fourball every golfer plays his own ball. In foursomes the two partners share one ball and hit alternate shots.

Is fourball the same as best ball?

Nearly. Fourball is the match-play version. Best ball usually means the same scoring counted as stroke play, a team total over 18 holes.

How are strokes given in fourball?

In Halved the default is strokes off the low: the lowest handicap of the four plays to scratch, and everyone else gets the difference on his own ball.

Can we play skins at the same time?

Yes. Skins, Stroke Play and Stableford can all run on the same four scores alongside a Fourball.
