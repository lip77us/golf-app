How to play Honors

One token goes round the group all day. The low score on a hole takes it, and you score a point for every hole you hold it. That is the whole game until two golfers tie, which is what most of the rules are about.

THE SHORT ANSWER

The honor goes to the lowest score on each hole. Hold it after a hole and you score a point. A tie never takes it from the holder. When golfers tie below the holder, earlier holes decide between them, and if they can’t, the honor goes loose.

The rules

There is one honor, and it is never split or shared. After every hole, somebody holds it or it is loose. The golfer holding it after a hole scores a point. At the end of the round, points are holes held.

1
Low score takes it
An outright low score on a hole takes the honor, or keeps it.
2
A tie doesn’t beat you
If the holder is in a tie for low, he keeps it.
3
Hold it, score it
Every hole you finish holding the honor is a point.
Why ties matter so much
In a group of four, many holes end with two or more golfers level for low. If a tie moved the honor, it would change hands on most holes. Because it doesn’t, a golfer who takes the honor has to be beaten outright to lose it.

The four tie cases

1
Outright low. Takes the honor, or keeps it. The ordinary hole.
2
Tie that includes the holder. The holder keeps it. Nobody scored strictly below him.
3
Tie among golfers who all beat the holder. Walk back through the earlier holes, most recent first, until one of them scored lower. He takes the honor.
Never separated: the honor dies
If the walk-back reaches the 1st without separating them, the honor goes loose. It does not go back to the holder, because he was beaten. A loose honor scores nobody a point, and the next outright low score or walk-back winner picks it up.

Handicaps

Net
Each golfer plays his course handicap at an allowance between 50% and 130%.
Strokes off the low man
The lowest handicap plays off scratch and everyone else gets the difference.
Gross
Scores straight off the card.

The money

One point is worth one stake, so a hole held is a hole paid. The points are settled in one of four ways:

vs Average
The default. You settle against the group’s average. Hold your share of the holes and you are square.
Above you
You pay each golfer who held more holes than you, and collect from each golfer who held fewer.
Just leader
Only the golfer who held the most collects. Everyone else pays him the difference.
Pool
Everyone antes the same and the pot splits by holes held.

Honors can also be a side bet between two or three golfers in a group. Anyone not playing can’t take the honor, and neither pays nor collects. Losses can be capped per golfer for the round.

Questions people ask

How do you win the honor in Honors?

Make the lowest score on a hole outright. If you are tied with others who all beat the current holder, earlier holes decide between you.

Does a tie take the honor from the holder?

No. If the holder is in the tie for low, he keeps it.

What does it mean when the honor dies?

Golfers who beat the holder were tied, and walking back through every earlier hole could not separate them. The honor goes loose and nobody scores a point until someone picks it up.

Can Honors be played net?

Yes. Net at an allowance from 50% to 130%, strokes off the low man, or gross.

Can only some of the group play Honors?

Yes. It can be a side bet between two or three golfers. Anyone not in it can't take the honor and neither pays nor collects.
